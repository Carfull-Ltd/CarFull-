CarFull Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

CarFull respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use CarFull, we may process your email address and account identifier, purchase and subscription information, vehicle information you save or check, Stories and other content you choose to upload, photos and documents you provide, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide vehicle checks and Car Histories, operate Garage and Social Hub features, process purchases and subscriptions, send essential account emails, provide support, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations and improve CarFull.

Public content

Content you deliberately publish to CarFull's Social Hub, including public Stories and public vehicle information, may be visible to other CarFull users. Private account information and documents are not made public merely because you use CarFull.

Sharing and service providers

We may share personal information with trusted providers only where necessary to operate CarFull. These may include Supabase for authentication and backend services, Resend for transactional email, Vercel for hosting, Apple and RevenueCat for in-app purchases and subscription management, Stripe where web payments are offered, and vehicle-data providers used to fulfil checks. These providers process information under their own contractual and legal obligations.

CarFull does not sell your personal information.

International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide CarFull, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and prevent abuse. Retention periods vary according to the type of information and the reason it is held.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information, object to certain processing and request portability where applicable. You may delete your CarFull account from the Account section of the app.

Safety and moderation

Users can report objectionable Social Hub content and block other users. Reports may be reviewed to enforce CarFull's rules and protect the community.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@carfull.co.uk.

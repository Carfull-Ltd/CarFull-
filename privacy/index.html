CarFull Privacy Policy

CarFull respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use CarFull.

Information we collect

When you create an account or use CarFull, we may process information such as your email address, account details, vehicle registrations, saved vehicles, vehicle history records, photographs, posts, comments and other content you choose to provide.

Vehicle information

CarFull retrieves vehicle information from third-party and public data sources. Vehicle registration numbers and related information may be processed to provide vehicle checks, MOT information and other CarFull features.

Public content

Content you choose to publish through community features such as Spotted or public Car Stories may be visible to other CarFull users. Please do not publish personal or sensitive information that you do not want to make public.

How we use information

Information is used to operate and improve CarFull, provide requested features, maintain accounts and subscriptions, prevent misuse, provide support and keep the service secure.

Payments and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions made through the iOS app are processed by Apple. CarFull does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Data storage

We use third-party service providers to help operate CarFull, including services for hosting, authentication, data storage and email delivery. Information may be processed by these providers only as necessary to provide the service.

Your choices

You can choose what information you add to CarFull and what content you publish. You may request deletion of your CarFull account and associated personal information, subject to any information we are legally required to retain.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by CarFull, although no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as CarFull develops. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact CarFull through our support channels at www.carfull.co.uk.
